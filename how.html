HOW IT WORKS

Two Squeeks in,
one stronger out.

On Squeekchain, two Squeeks of the same level merge into one Squeek a level higher, with 10% more power than the pair. The catch: you need that second Squeek. Merge Market is built to find it.

Three kinds of order

  • For sale: list a Squeek for ETH. It stays in your wallet until someone buys it.
  • Swap: offer your Squeek for a specific Squeek, or for any Squeek of a level. When someone accepts, both Squeeks change hands in the same transaction.
  • Bid: put up ETH for any Squeek of a level ("I need a Level 3"). The ETH waits in the contract until someone sells into it, or you cancel and take it back.

Finding a partner

  • The site reads your Squeeks (in your wallet and in Staking), their levels and merge cooldowns.
  • Pairs that can merge now get a one-click merge button. In your wallet it calls SqueekNFT.merge; for two staked Squeeks it calls SqueekStaking.mergeDeposited.
  • A Squeek without a partner shows how many matching Squeeks are on the market, and lets you bid or offer a swap.
  • Before you trade, the site shows the power you would have after merging, read from Staking's basePower.

Safety

  • Non-custodial: Squeeks never sit in the contract. A trade moves them straight from one wallet to the other, so listings cost nothing to keep and can't be lost.
  • Ownerless: no admin key, no pause, no upgrade. The contract is verified on Sourcify, so anyone can read the exact code that runs.
  • Fee-free: Merge Market takes nothing. The collection's ERC-2981 royalty is paid on ETH sales, capped at 10%.
  • Stale orders can't hurt you: if a maker moves, merges or un-approves their Squeek, their order simply stops being fillable. A trade either completes in full or not at all.
  • Approving the market lets it move your Squeeks only as part of a trade you or your counterparty sign. You can revoke the approval any time.
  • Staked Squeeks are held by Staking and can't be traded; withdraw them first. A Squeek's level and merge cooldown travel with it; earned rewards stay with the seller.

Open source

Contract, tests and site: github.com/0xdogacan/squeek-merge-market. The contract test suite covers sales, swaps, bids, royalties, expiry, stale orders, reentrancy and payout fallbacks.
